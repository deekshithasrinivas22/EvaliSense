System Overview & Roles

EvaliSense is an AI-assisted handwritten examination evaluation management system with full human-in-the-loop review. Sign in using your role credentials below to access role-specific workflows.

🏛️ HOD / Administrator

Manages users, custom roles, departments, sections, students, subjects, exams, answer keys, rubrics, and inspects results.

👩‍🏫 Teacher / Examiner

Inspects assigned high-risk answers side-by-side with handwriting image, OCR text, rubric, and submits final marks.

📠 Scanner Operator

Uploads answer sheets, monitors image quality validation, blur detection, OCR confidence, and manages rescans.
